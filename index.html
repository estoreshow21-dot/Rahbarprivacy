Welcome to Rahbar ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and personal data of our riders, driver partners, and courier customers. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is processed and shared, and the rights you have over your data when using the Rahbar mobile application and related online services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable transportation and parcel delivery, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Location Information (Prominent Disclosure)

⚠️ Crucial Location Data Notice:
Rahbar collects location data to enable trip dispatch, route navigation, live driver tracking on the customer map, and accurate fare calculation based on travel distance.
  • For Customers/Riders: We collect precise and approximate GPS location when the app is active and in foreground to set your pickup location and track your ongoing trip.
  • For Driver Partners: We collect precise GPS location data both in foreground and background (even when the app is closed or running in the notification bar) while you are in the "Online" state. This is required so nearby passengers can locate your vehicle, calculate estimated arrival times, and navigate the trip safely.

B. Personal & Account Details

  • Registration Info: Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, and Email Address.
  • Driver Partner Documents: CNIC/ID details, Driving License photos, Vehicle Registration number, and Vehicle Type (Bike, Auto Rickshaw, Car) to verify driver identity and passenger safety.
  • Profile Picture: Optional user avatar or driver profile photo.

C. Trip, Transaction & Wallet Data

  • Pickup and drop-off coordinates, distance traveled, timestamps, vehicle type chosen, and fare amounts.
  • In-app wallet balance, top-up records (via JazzCash / EasyPaisa), and driver withdrawal requests.

D. Communications & Support

  • Customer support queries, message history with administrators, and feedback submitted via in-app forms or official WhatsApp helpline.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Matching & Booking: Connecting customers with the nearest available drivers.
  • Navigation & Tracking: Displaying real-time driver movement on the map and calculating trip fare.
  • Safety & Verification: Using One-Time Passwords (OTP) to start trips, confirming driver credentials, and offering emergency SOS tools.
  • Customer Support: Responding to inquiries, resolving billing disputes, and managing complaints.
  • Platform Security: Preventing fraud, unauthorized access, and malicious activities.

3. How We Share Your Information

We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or commercial marketers. We only share data under the following circumstances:

  • Between Customer and Driver: Once a ride is matched, the driver sees the customer's pickup name and location; the customer sees the driver's name, vehicle details, rating, and live location.
  • Trusted Service Providers:
    • Google Firebase: For real-time database sync, user authentication, and secure cloud storage.
    • Google Maps Platform: For map rendering, reverse geocoding, and distance calculations.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by Pakistani law enforcement agencies, court orders, or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to maintain your account and comply with safety and legal records.

🗑️ User Right to Delete Account and Data:
You have the complete right to delete your account and all associated trip history at any time:
  1. In-App Deletion: Open the Rahbar App → Navigate to Account (اکاؤنٹ) tab → Tap Delete Account (اکاؤنٹ ڈیلیٹ کریں) → Confirm deletion.
  2. Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to rahberride7@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account" and your phone number. Your account and personal data will be permanently wiped within 7 business days.

5. Security of Your Personal Data

The security of your personal information is a top priority. All communications between the Rahbar app and our servers utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption. Access to user records is strictly restricted to authorized administrative personnel.

6. Children's Privacy

Rahbar is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from anyone under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted here with the effective date updated. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
